This a game for the All In Pixel Jam #2. It is about a young man, finding himself on a weird kind of world full of infected ground. He is locked in a facility which is trying to find out more about the mysterious outbreak of the infection, as this world was once beautiful even more than the young man's so-called "earth". Try to find a way out and convince these people to help them recover this world to it's former state and get back home.
